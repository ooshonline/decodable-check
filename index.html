Fix-it suggestions

How it reads words: Decodable Check breaks each word into graphemes (letter–sound spellings), then checks every grapheme against the skills you've ticked. A word turns green only when all of its spellings have been taught. Common irregular words are matched against a built-in heart-word list. It's a fast classroom heuristic, not a substitute for your own professional judgement — always skim the amber words yourself.

Pick from the word bank

Tick the words that fit your theme. These words know what goes with them (a pink pig, a duck that swims), so they can go in any sentence. Faded words aren’t decodable with these skills yet — they’re skipped until they are.

Add your own words

Your own words only go in simple sentences that suit any word — Sam has a ___. · Sam fed the ___. · The ___ can nap. · Sam was in the ___. — and never as plurals. A word that isn’t decodable yet is still used, but it shows amber so you can pre-teach it.
