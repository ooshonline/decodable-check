Fix-it suggestions

How it reads words: Decodable Check breaks each word into graphemes (letter–sound spellings), then checks every grapheme against the skills you've ticked. A word turns green only when all of its spellings have been taught. Common irregular words are matched against a built-in heart-word list. It's a fast classroom heuristic, not a substitute for your own professional judgement — always skim the amber words yourself.
